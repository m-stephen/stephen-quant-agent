# V2.7 M1 — Point-in-Time Readiness Audit

## Goal

Turn previously frozen, pre-2025 metadata into a source-specific authorization matrix before any new alpha research begins.

## Integrity boundary

- Read only the committed source contract and previously recorded evidence hashes.
- Do not enumerate raw-data directories or read 2025/2026 data.
- Do not calculate returns, IC, placebo statistics, or backtests.
- Preserve the cumulative inferential-trial count at 48 and make no remote-model requests.
- Treat missing publication, revision, membership, or survivorship provenance as not research-ready.

## Acceptance

The audit must emit JSON plus English/Chinese reports and an offline replay manifest. Only explicitly authorized price-derived controls may enter M2. Industry indices must never authorize stock-level industry membership.
