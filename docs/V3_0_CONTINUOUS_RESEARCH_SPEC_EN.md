# V3.0 Continuous Factor Research Loop

## Objective

Explore genuinely new economic mechanisms in frozen research epochs without weakening Alpha Court
or opening the 2025/2026 sealed windows. Failed candidates become tombstones; later epochs must
change the mechanism instead of tuning the same formula after observing its result.

## Research epochs

- Research window: 2022-01-04 through 2024-12-31.
- Each epoch permits three schemas, three CPCV configurations and three cost-aware executions.
- Preregistered mechanisms progress through demand acceleration, crowding/liquidity, chip levels,
  chip dynamics and limit-up events.
- Multiplicity: inherit 52 inferential trials through V2.9 and add every epoch attempt.
- Execution: CNY 3m with explicit commission, stamp duty, slippage, impact and a 5% ADV cap.

## Immutable gates

- training mean RankIC at least 0.005 and CPCV mean path RankIC at least 0.015;
- at least eight positive CPCV paths and PBO no greater than 0.05;
- both placebo p-values no greater than 0.05 and DSR at least 0.95;
- positive cost-aware walk-forward performance;
- CNY 3m annualized net Sharpe at least 0.50 and maximum drawdown no worse than -25%;
- zero access to 2025/2026.

Only a newly preregistered economic mechanism may follow a failed epoch. Exhausting the current
data/mechanism budget returns `NO_ALPHA_IN_CURRENT_MECHANISM_SET` and stops the loop. Alpha Court
passage is research evidence only and still requires independent validation plus capacity and risk
review.
